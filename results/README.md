# Results

These are the raw result files behind the tables in the paper. Each one is written by the script listed in the main README. They are licensed under CC BY 4.0.

`nist_full.json` holds the 100-sequence runs on the baseline keystreams. Its "Serial" entry is the minimum of the two Serial p-values. The paper reports the two Serial sub-tests separately, from `serial_fix.json`.
